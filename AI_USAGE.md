
## AI Usage & Policy Compliance

AI tools may be used to assist in the development and maintenance of Accurate Judgement Bar, including code analysis, debugging, implementation, and documentation.

When processing intellectual property belonging to 7th Beat Games, AI tools are used only with model training disabled or under a zero data retention policy.

All AI-generated code incorporated into this project is subject to human review and appropriate testing before release.

This project is not intended to replace, recreate, or reimagine A Dance of Fire and Ice.

## Disclaimer

Accurate Judgement Bar is an unofficial third-party modification for A Dance of Fire and Ice. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by 7th Beat Games.

This mod is intended to improve the accuracy of the visual representation of judgement windows. It does not modify the game's actual judgement windows, hit judgements, or scoring mechanics.

Mods and mod loaders may cause crashes, instability, or compatibility issues. Users are advised to remove their mod loader after a game update and reinstall it once compatibility has been confirmed.

7th Beat Games is not responsible for any issues arising from the use of this mod.
